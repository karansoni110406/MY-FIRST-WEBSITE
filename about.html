Jaipur Engineering College Kukas

Jaipur Engineering College Kukas, also known as JEC Kukas, is an engineering college located in the RIICO Industrial Area of Jaipur, Rajasthan.

The college was established in 2000 and offers engineering education in various specializations. Students can explore technical learning, academic programs and career opportunities.

For more information, visit the official JEC Kukas website .
